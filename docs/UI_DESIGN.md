# UI design

The Bento Grid design system applies to the admin panel only: login, dashboard, blog, email, and contact-submission screens use `public/css/admin.css` with responsive cards, admin tokens, light/dark mode, focus states, and status feedback.

Public frontend pages follow the existing Make My Tours & Travels theme: Heebo body text, Barlow headings, orange accents, image banners, custom page styles, and the shared header/footer. Public blog listing, category, tag, and detail views use the shared header and footer, site fonts, and blog-scoped styles. Listing and article banners follow the inner-page image treatment, dark overlay, breadcrumb, and responsive spacing.

Blog responsive rules use the site's 1100 px, 1000 px, 800 px, 720 px, 600 px, and 480 px breakpoints. At 1100 px the blog header changes to a compact header with an accessible menu and a fitting phone action; at 1000 px banners and article text become fluid and article content/sidebar stack; at 800 px listing cards use two columns; and at 600 px cards and footer columns use one column. Images preserve their aspect ratio, long article text wraps, and code/tables scroll within their content area. Maintain 16 px article/card body text and 44 px navigation and action targets at tablet and phone widths. Keep blog-specific styling scoped to `.blog-body` and preserve accessible labels, keyboard focus, and mobile reflow. Public pages do not use admin or Bento styling.
